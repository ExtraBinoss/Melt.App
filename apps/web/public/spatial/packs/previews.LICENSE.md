# Pack thumbnails

The hash-named `previews-*.webp` atlas contains native Melt renders of the 474
Kenney Nature Kit and Prototype Kit models. Models and original palette textures
are CC0-1.0; their original licenses are retained in `Kenney-nature.txt` and
`Kenney-prototype.txt`, with per-model provenance in the pinned pack manifest.

- https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- https://kenney.nl/assets/prototype-kit

Reproduce the atlas with `bun scripts/spatial/render-pack.ts`. Camera, lighting and
composition use Melt's shared native library preview document.
